BlazeWatchComing 2027

Wildfire awareness for all of Europe, in one app — satellite-detected fires and proximity alerts for the places you care about, wherever you are.

2025 was the worst EU wildfire season on record — over one million hectares burned (JRC). If your holiday home, family, or favourite hiking region is in a fire-prone part of Europe, "is everything okay over there?" deserves a better answer than scanning local news in a language you don't read.

  • Pan-European — one app, no national borders where the data stops
  • Satellite detections from NASA and Copernicus, roughly ten passes a day
  • Multi-layer false-alarm filtering — when BlazeWatch pings, it means something
  • Proximity alerts for your saved place — a holiday home 2,000 km away, or the town your family lives in
  • Live fire map with a Copernicus fire-danger overlay
  • Free: full map and alerts for one saved place — a small one-time unlock for more. No subscription.
  • English-first, built in the EU

BlazeWatch is an awareness tool, not an emergency service. In an emergency, always follow the instructions of local authorities. Fire data: NASA FIRMS and Copernicus EFFIS.
