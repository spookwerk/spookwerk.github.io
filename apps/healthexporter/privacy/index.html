Privacy Policy

Effective: 28 February 2026

Health Exporter is developed by Spookwerk. This policy explains how the app handles your data. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we have no access to it.

Data Collection

Health Exporter does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. The app has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, and no advertising. It does not require an internet connection to function.

Health Data

The app reads health data from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission. This data is used solely to generate export files (JSON and CSV) that are stored locally on your device. Specifically:

  • Health data is read from HealthKit only after you grant permission
  • Export files are saved to your device's local storage by default
  • You may optionally choose a custom export folder (such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or other providers accessible through the iOS Files app) — this is entirely your choice and your responsibility
  • The app never uploads health data to any server owned or operated by Spookwerk

Data Storage

All exported data is stored locally on your device by default, in compliance with Apple's guidelines for health data. If you choose a cloud-based export folder, the data is transferred to that provider under their terms and privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

Health Exporter offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Spookwerk does not receive or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party SDKs, libraries, or services. All functionality is built using Apple's native frameworks.

Children's Privacy

Health Exporter is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's update notes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to privacy@spookwerk.app.
