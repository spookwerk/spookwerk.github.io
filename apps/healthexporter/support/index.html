Support

If you have a question, found a bug, or need help with Health Exporter, send an email to support@spookwerk.app.

Common Questions

  • Where are my exported files? By default, exports are saved to the app's local storage on your device. You can find them in the Files app under On My iPhone > Health Exporter > HealthExports. You can also choose a custom folder (like iCloud Drive) in Settings.
  • Why does the app need access to all health data types? Health Exporter requests read access to all available types so you can export your complete health history. You can disable categories you don't need in Settings > Export Categories.
  • How do I restore my purchase? Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases".

Privacy

Health Exporter does not collect any personal data. All processing happens on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
