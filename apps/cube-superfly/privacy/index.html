Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective: 20 September 2026

Cube Superfly for iOS is developed and published by Spookwerk, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) registered in the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce / KvK number 42035025, registered office: De Nieuwe Erven 3, Unit 14531, 5431 NV Cuijk, Netherlands). The data controller under the GDPR is Spookwerk, reachable at privacy@spookwerk.app.

This policy explains how the app handles your data. The short version: everything stays on your device. Spookwerk does not access, cannot access, and will not access your data.

Data We Do Not Collect

Cube Superfly does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no account system, no tracking, and no crash reporting. Spookwerk runs no server for this app, and the app does not send anything to Spookwerk, under any circumstance.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your progress, settings, and local high scores — including the initials you type in for a high score — on your device, inside the app's own sandboxed container. This data is protected by iOS's standard device-level encryption. It is deleted automatically when you delete the app. If Cube Superfly 1.02 was previously installed on this device, its save data is imported once, the first time version 2.0 launches.

Game Center

Cube Superfly offers an optional Game Center leaderboard. Game Center is Apple's service, governed by Apple's own privacy terms (see https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/). If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score from each finished run is submitted to the "High score" leaderboard under your Game Center profile. To stop this, sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings. Spookwerk never receives any Game Center data — scores are submitted directly from your device to Apple.

Third-Party Services

Cube Superfly uses no third-party services besides Apple's Game Center, described above. The game is built with the Unity engine; Unity's own engine services — analytics, advertising, and crash reporting — are disabled in this app.

Children

Cube Superfly is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either, and no parental consent mechanism is needed.

Your Rights

Because Spookwerk holds no personal data about you — everything lives only on your device or with Apple's Game Center — there is, in practice, no data at Spookwerk to exercise these rights against. For completeness, the GDPR rights are:

  • Right of access (Article 15) — Your data is stored on your device. You have direct, unrestricted access to it at all times.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16) — Not applicable: Spookwerk holds no data to rectify. Your local high scores are reset by deleting the app.
  • Right to erasure (Article 17) — Uninstalling the app deletes all local app data immediately.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Article 18) — Since no data leaves your device, there is no server-side processing to restrict.
  • Right to data portability (Article 20) — There is no controller-held copy to port, since your data never leaves your device and Spookwerk processes nothing.
  • Right to object (Article 21) — No processing occurs for direct marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.
  • Right to withdraw consent — You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time to stop future score submissions to Apple.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — You may file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

Security Updates

Spookwerk provides security updates for Cube Superfly until at least 20 September 2028, two years from the release of version 2.0 on the App Store. Updates are delivered through the App Store at no additional cost. This is a minimum, not a maximum.

Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Cube Superfly or in any Spookwerk service, please report it to security@spookwerk.app. We follow a coordinated vulnerability disclosure process: we confirm receipt, investigate, and keep you informed of the resolution. Machine-readable details are published in our security.txt.

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to privacy@spookwerk.app.
